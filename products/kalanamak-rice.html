THE BUDDHA RICE STORY

A Legacy Dating Back to 600 BC

Legend has it that Lord Buddha, after breaking his fast at Kapilvastu, gifted these black-husked grains to the local villagers, asking them to sow them. The resulting rice possessed a unique aroma, carrying the essence of Buddha's mercy. Today, BuddhaAgro combines this ancient heritage with modern precision agriculture to produce the purest, most aromatic Kalanamak rice.
